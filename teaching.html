My views about Teaching

Inspired by my parents, both teachers, I developed a passion for teaching from an early age. Mathematics came naturally to me, creating opportunities to discuss concepts with peers and share my understanding. Exploring Gauss’s work on sequences, telescoping sums, and visualizing functions through graphs deepened my appreciation for mathematical discovery and inspired me to pursue a similar path of inquiry. These experiences strengthened my belief that teaching plays a vital role in nurturing the next generation of critical thinkers.
