Research Interests

Stochastic Processes, Hidden Markov Models, Parameter Estimation, and Adversarial Machine Learning.

Research Work

Lévy Processes and Telegraph Signals

Parametric modeling and numerical estimation for Lévy processes governed by telegraph signals. Ongoing work; published in Statistical Inference for Stochastic Processes.

Adversarial Machine Learning

Modeling the success and failure of adversarial examples against defense ensembles and real-world systems, including elections, and estimating parameters that capture relevant attributes. Ongoing work.
